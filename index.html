      Claude check

      Optional. Paste your Claude API key to have Claude score each saved shot against listing-photo standards and your references. The key is kept only in this browser and is sent only to Anthropic. It is billed to your API account, not your Claude plan.

      Privacy & terms

      Privacy

      The live camera view, motion readings and photos are processed on your phone and are not uploaded. No accounts, analytics or ads. The exceptions are the optional Claude features, which use your own key: the Claude check sends that photo and your reference photos to Anthropic's API, and the VIN reader sends the VIN photo. A VIN you confirm is also sent to the U.S. NHTSA's free decoder to look up the year, make and model. The VIN is not stored after you close the tab. Saved photos carry no location or other metadata.

      Third parties

      Four, each for one purpose: jsDelivr serves the open-source TensorFlow.js code that finds the car, Google Cloud Storage serves that model's file, Anthropic answers the Claude check and reads the VIN if you add a key, and NHTSA decodes a VIN. No other SDKs or trackers.

      Cookies and storage

      No cookies. Your kept photos, your auto-shoot choice, and your API key if you add one, are kept in this browser's own storage so the tool works next time. Nothing optional is stored, so there is no consent banner.

      Terms of use

      The coaching is guidance, not a guarantee of a sale or a price. The lighting fix changes exposure only. Do not use edits to hide damage or misdescribe a vehicle; you are responsible for what your listing shows. Watch your surroundings while you move around the car.

      Price and refunds

      This tool is free, with no fees and nothing to refund. The optional Claude check is billed by Anthropic to your own API account.

      Delete your data

      Photos you keep are stored in this browser on your phone, so a refresh does not lose them. They are never uploaded. Start a new car deletes them, and the button below wipes them along with your saved settings and API key.

      Children

      Not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

      Email

      This tool sends no email, so there is nothing to unsubscribe from.

      Accessibility

      Works with a keyboard and screen reader, and each live instruction is announced. Tell the operator if something is hard to use.
